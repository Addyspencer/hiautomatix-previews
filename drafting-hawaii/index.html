Frequently asked questions

Are you able to route my permit?

Yes we can! We are able to include permit routing services in our proposal if you request it.

How much are your services?

That depends on a few things: whether your home is up to date in terms of permitting, whether there are record drawings or existing floor plans (to see if field surveying is needed), and what you plan to do in terms of renovation.
